**Tweet 1 (hook)**

Freelancing is broken: clients fear losing deposits, freelancers fear not getting paid. 🤝

Meet WorkVault — a trustless freelance escrow built on Stellar Soroban that fixes both.

No middleman. No custody risk. No platform holding your money. 🧵

**Tweet 2 (the problem)**

When you hire a freelancer online today you either:
• Prepay and pray 🤞
• Wire first and hope they deliver

Freelancers have the opposite fear — finish the work, never see a dime.

Both sides lose trust. Work gets done in spite of the platforms, not because of them.

**Tweet 3 (the idea)**

WorkVault puts money in a smart contract, not in anyone's pocket.

Client deposits into a vault → milestones are agreed on-chain → freelancer submits proof of work → client approves → funds release instantly.

Every step is verifiable on Stellar. No take-backs, no disappearing deposits.

**Tweet 4 (portable reputation)**

Here's the part we're most proud of. 🏆

Every completed contract writes a permanent work-history record that the FREELANCER owns — portable across platforms.

You're not building reputation for someone else's website. You own it. Forever.

**Tweet 5 (2% platform fee, how we make money)**

How do we make money? A flat 2% platform fee on completed contracts.

No subscriptions. No pay-to-play listings. WorkVault only earns when you do.

That keeps our incentives aligned with yours.

**Tweet 6 (multi-sig milestone)**

New: two-party release approval (multi-sig). 🔐

Neither party releases a milestone alone — both the client AND freelancer must approve the release.

That's real two-sided trust, enforced by the contract, not by words.
